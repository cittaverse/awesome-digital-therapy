**Published Version**:

## Study Design

| Dimension | Design |
|-----------|--------|
| **Study Type** | Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT) |
| **Sample Size** | N = 540 (adults with elevated anxiety/depression symptoms) |
| **Intervention** | Limbic Care App (genAI-personalized CBT content) |
| **Control** | Static PDF CBT workbook (standard care) |
| **Duration** | 6 weeks |
| **Primary Endpoints** | Engagement metrics + symptom improvement |
| **Trial Registration** | NCT06459128 |

---

## Key Findings

### Engagement (Primary Outcome)

| Metric | Intervention | Control | Effect Size |
|--------|--------------|---------|-------------|
| **Usage Frequency** | 2.4× higher | Baseline | p < 0.001 |
| **Usage Duration** | 3.8× longer | Baseline | p < 0.001 |
| **Overall Engagement** | 3× improvement | Baseline | p < 0.001 |

### Clinical Outcomes

| Outcome | Intervention | Control | Significance |
|---------|--------------|---------|--------------|
| **Overall Symptom Improvement** | Similar | Similar | NS |
| **Anxiety Symptoms** (users of personalized features) | Significantly reduced | - | p < 0.05 |
| **Well-being** (users of personalized features) | Significantly improved | - | p < 0.05 |
| **Safety** | No increase in adverse events | - | NS |

### Key Insight

> **"Engagement with clinically personalized features"** was the critical mediator:
> - Users who engaged with genAI-personalized content showed significant anxiety reduction and well-being improvement
> - Overall ITT analysis showed similar symptom improvement (likely due to engagement variability)
> - **Conclusion**: genAI personalization → higher engagement → better outcomes for engaged users

---

## Mechanism of Action

```
genAI Personalization
        ↓
  Higher Engagement (3×)
        ↓
  More CBT Content Exposure
        ↓
  Better Clinical Outcomes (for engaged users)
```

**Key Differentiators**:
- **Dynamic personalization**: AI adapts CBT content to user's specific symptoms, preferences, and progress
- **Conversational interface**: More engaging than static workbooks
- **Just-in-time support**: Available on-demand vs. scheduled therapy sessions

## Limitations (Critical Appraisal)

| Limitation | Impact | Mitigation for CittaVerse |
|------------|--------|---------------------------|
| **Self-selected sample** | May overestimate engagement | Track recruitment channel effects |
| **Short duration (6 weeks)** | Unknown long-term effects | Include 3-month follow-up in design |
| **No active control** | Cannot separate personalization from novelty | Consider active control in future RCT |
| **Adult population** | Not generalizable to elderly | Pilot specifically in 60-80 age group |
| **Self-reported outcomes** | Potential bias | Include objective cognitive measures (MoCA) |

## Actionable Insights for CittaVerse Pilot

### ✅ Adopt from Limbic

   - Track session duration (minutes/session)
   - Track feature utilization (which guidance types used)

2. **Safety Monitoring**:
   - Adverse event tracking (daily)
   - Emotional distress monitoring (session-level)
   - Clear escalation pathway

3. **Personalization Strategy**:
   - Meta-memory guidance tailored to narrative content
   - Adaptive difficulty (based on MoCA baseline)
   - Flexible scheduling (accommodate daily routines)

### ⚠️ Adapt for Elderly Population

1. **Interface Design**:
   - Larger fonts, simpler navigation
   - Voice-first interaction (not text-heavy)
   - Family/caregiver support option

2. **Content Focus**:
   - Reminiscence (not CBT)
   - Life review and meaning-making
   - Social connection emphasis

3. **Outcome Measures**:
   - Cognitive function (MoCA) primary
   - Narrative quality (6 dimensions)
   - Quality of life (QOL-AD)
